To help you achieve a high-quality outcome, I evaluate projects based on how effectively the technical implementation solves the user’s problem. For this specific story-branching app, a "good score" would be based on these four pillars:

Functional Integrity: Does the AI consistently generate logical continuations? Does the "combine into final draft" feature correctly stitch the chosen path together without losing data?

User Experience (UX): Is the story-tree intuitive to navigate? A user should be able to look at the branch view and immediately understand where they are and how to backtrack to a previous decision.

Prompt Engineering: How well have you instructed the AI to provide distinct plot directions? If the 2-3 choices are too similar, the tool loses its brainstorming value.

State Management: Can the app handle complex branching without getting "confused" about which scene belongs to which plot line?
